Now

My current work asks what a driving stack should fall back on when a camera goes dark. In ReSMap the answer is geo-aligned satellite imagery: it is cached offline, so no onboard fault can take it away, and a confidence-gated fusion lets the map survive losing all six cameras.

Alongside that I look at how multi-task driving models actually spend their capacity — which modality a fusion network leans on, whether a forecaster is right for the right reasons, and how module-wise distillation transfers a perception backbone into an end-to-end planner without the tasks fighting each other for gradients.
